# Graywood Scandinavian Editorial Design System

## 1. Palette Tokens (Tailwind)
- Background/Canvas: `#F9F8F6` (`nordic-canvas`)
- Cards/Surfaces: `#FFFFFF` (`nordic-surface`)
- Muted/Pill Backgrounds: `#F1EFEA` (`nordic-muted`)
- Hairline Borders: `#E8E5DF` (`nordic-border`)
- Section Dividers: `#DDD9D0` (`nordic-divider`)
- Primary Ink/Text: `#1C1B19` (`nordic-ink`)
- Secondary Subtle Text: `#68655E` (`nordic-subtle`)
- Metadata/Faint: `#9E9B93` (`nordic-faint`)
- Primary Accent (Nordic Pine): `#2D3B36` (`nordic-pine`)
- Secondary Accent (Terracotta): `#C86D51` (`nordic-clay`)

## 2. Typography Rules
- Headlines & Hero Titles: Editorial Serif (`Playfair Display`, `Cinzel`, or standard system serif).
- Body & UI Text: Modern Sans (`Inter`).
- Metadata, Telemetry, Dates, Counters: Monospace (`JetBrains Mono` or `font-mono`).

## 3. Strict Consistency Rule
Do NOT use pitch black (`#000000`, `bg-zinc-950`) or high-contrast neon accents anywhere on the site. All portals (`graywood.no`, `graywoodphotography.no`, `graywoodmedia.no`, and `/admin`) must share this warm, unbleached limestone and archival paper aesthetic.
